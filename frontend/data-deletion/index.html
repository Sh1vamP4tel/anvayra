Data Deletion

Last updated: 24 October 2026 · This page explains how to have your personal data deleted from Anvayra’s systems.

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data. This includes data submitted through our website, WhatsApp conversations handled by an Anvayra automation, and records connected to a WhatsApp Business Account.

Fastest way to request deletion:
Email shivamkushwahsironj@gmail.com with the subject “Delete My Data”. We confirm receipt within 2 working days and complete deletion within 7 days.

Already requested deletion? Check the status

If you already received a confirmation code (from Meta or from our team), you can check the live status of your request at any time.

Don’t have a code? Request deletion by email above or via the deletion status page.

What to include in your request

  • Your full name.
  • The WhatsApp phone number or email address used to contact us.
  • The business name, if you contacted us on behalf of a business.
  • Whether you want all data deleted, or only a specific conversation or record.

We may ask for one verification step to make sure we do not delete the wrong person’s data. We never ask for your Meta password.

What we delete

  • Contact-form submissions, including name, email, phone and message.
  • WhatsApp message logs and delivery records stored by our automation.
  • Opt-in and conversation metadata associated with your number.
  • Dashboard session records and any support notes tied to your enquiry.

What may be retained

  • Suppression record: if you opted out with “STOP”, we keep only the minimum identifier needed to avoid messaging you again.
  • Legal records: invoices or tax records we are required to keep under Indian law.
  • Security logs: limited technical logs retained for a short period to investigate abuse or fraud.

Anything retained is kept only for the required period and is not used for marketing.

Deleting data held by Meta or WhatsApp

Anvayra can delete the data it stores, but we cannot delete data inside your own Meta or WhatsApp account. To manage or delete that data:

  • Use Meta Privacy Center for Facebook account data.
  • Use WhatsApp’s in-app Settings → Account → Delete my account for WhatsApp data.
  • Remove Anvayra’s access in Meta Business Manager → Business Settings → Integrations if you no longer want us connected.

Disconnecting the integration

You can disconnect your WhatsApp Business Account from Anvayra at any time:

  1. Open the client dashboard and click Disconnect locally.
  2. Email us with the subject “Delete My Data” to remove backend records.
  3. Optionally remove the Anvayra app from your Meta Business integrations.
