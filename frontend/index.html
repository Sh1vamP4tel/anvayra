Security & compliance
  • SecretsApp Secret and access tokens live only in the encrypted backend. They are never sent to the browser.
  • ConsentContacts are messaged only after opt-in. The website form stores an explicit WhatsApp consent checkbox.
  • STOPAn incoming “STOP” opts the contact out immediately; marketing messages are suppressed.
  • RetentionMessage logs are kept for 90 days for support, then deleted unless the law requires longer.
  • DeletionAny person can request deletion by email; requests are actioned within 7 days.
Reviewer instructions

Test the full flow in 5 minutes

  1. Sign in with the reviewer account supplied in the App Review submission form. Review credentials are never published on this website.
  2. Open Settings → Connect WhatsApp Business and complete Meta Embedded Signup to link a WhatsApp Business Account and phone number.
  3. Open Inbox to read real customer conversations delivered by the Cloud API webhook, and reply from the composer. Sent messages are recorded with the Cloud API wamid.
  4. Approved message templates from the connected WABA appear under Templates. Automation switches under Automations are stored per account.
  5. Every inbound webhook is verified with X-Hub-Signature-256 HMAC before anything is stored. Delivery status events update each message.
