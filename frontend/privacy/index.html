Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026 · Applies to anvayra.vercel.app and the Anvayra WhatsApp automation service.

Anvayra (“Anvayra”, “we”, “us”) is a WhatsApp Business automation studio operated by Shivam Kushwah, a sole proprietor based in Sironj, Madhya Pradesh, India. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how it is used, and the choices you have.

In short: we collect only what is needed to reply to enquiries and to operate WhatsApp automation for our clients. We do not sell personal data. We do not use Meta Platform Data for advertising. You can request deletion at any time.

Anvayra provides WhatsApp automation, AI agents and workflow automation to businesses. We act as a data processor for the customer data our clients send through their connected WhatsApp Business Account, and as a data controller for enquiries sent to us directly.

2. Data we collect

From website visitors and enquiries

  • Name, business name, email address and WhatsApp phone number you submit in our contact form.
  • The service you are interested in and the message you write.
  • Your WhatsApp consent confirmation and the date/time it was given.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited, used only to keep the site working and secure.

From clients using the dashboard

  • Meta Business and WhatsApp Business Account identifiers (WABA ID, Phone Number ID), returned by Meta Embedded Signup.
  • Access tokens issued by Meta, stored encrypted in our backend. They are never sent to the browser.
  • Message logs, template names and delivery statuses needed to operate and support the service.

From people who message a business using Anvayra

  • WhatsApp profile name, phone number, message content and timestamps.
  • Opt-in and opt-out (STOP) status.

3. Social sign-in (Google & Facebook OAuth)

Our workspace sign-in offers Google OAuth and, where enabled, Facebook Login alongside standard email and password. If you choose social sign-in:

  • Authentication is handled by Google or Facebook (Meta) and by Supabase, our authentication and database provider. We never see or store your Google/Facebook password.
  • We receive your name, email address and profile picture from the provider (scopes email and profile / public_profile). We use them only to create and identify your workspace account.
  • We do not post anything to your Google or Facebook account, and we do not use social data for advertising.
  • You can disconnect the social link by signing in with email/password and contacting us, or by requesting deletion (see Data Deletion).

Dashboard accounts are stored in Supabase (project nbxsebcrcussmmxismsa), which hosts our production database, authentication and serverless functions.

4. WhatsApp and Meta Platform Data

Anvayra uses the official WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API) and Meta Embedded Signup. When a client connects an account, Meta provides us with the identifiers and permissions needed to send and receive messages on that client’s behalf.

Our use of Meta Platform Data is limited to providing the services requested by the client. Specifically:

  • We do not sell Meta Platform Data.
  • We do not use WhatsApp message content for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not transfer Meta Platform Data to data brokers or unrelated third parties.
  • We access only the business assets the client selects during Embedded Signup.

Meta’s own handling of data is governed by the Meta Privacy Policy and the WhatsApp Business Terms.

5. How we use data

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss our services.
  • To connect a client’s WhatsApp Business Account and send the messages the client instructs.
  • To deliver automated replies, reminders, follow-ups and template campaigns on behalf of a client.
  • To provide support, troubleshoot delivery failures and maintain an audit log.
  • To meet legal, tax and Meta platform compliance obligations.

We rely on your consent for WhatsApp communication, on the performance of our contract with clients, and on our legitimate interest in operating a secure service.

6. Sharing and sub-processors

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with providers needed to deliver the service:

  • Meta Platforms / WhatsApp — to send and receive messages through the Cloud API.
  • Supabase — authentication, database and Edge Functions (webhooks, token vault, message sending).
  • Hosting provider — serves this static website and its logs.
  • AI model providers — only where a client has requested an AI agent; message content needed to generate a reply may be processed by the configured model provider.

We may disclose data if required by law, court order or a valid regulatory request, or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

7. Data retention

  • Enquiries: up to 24 months from the last contact, unless you ask us to delete earlier.
  • Message logs: 90 days for support and delivery troubleshooting, then deleted.
  • Consent records: retained while the contact remains opted in, plus a suppression record after opt-out so we do not message them again.
  • Billing and tax records: retained as required by Indian law.

8. Your rights and choices

You may ask us to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, or withdraw your WhatsApp consent. To exercise any of these rights:

  • Email shivamkushwahsironj@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy Request”.
  • Reply STOP to any WhatsApp message to opt out of further marketing messages.
  • Follow the steps on our Data Deletion page.

We respond within 30 days, and action verified deletion requests within 7 days. You may also complain to the relevant data protection authority in your jurisdiction.

9. Security

  • App Secret and access tokens are stored only in encrypted credentials, never in the website code.
  • All traffic between the website and the backend uses HTTPS.
  • Access to the backend is restricted to authorised personnel and protected by account-level authentication.
  • We keep the amount of data we collect to the minimum needed to run the service.

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting personal data, we will notify affected users and the relevant authorities without undue delay.

10. Children

Our services are intended for businesses and are not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. International transfers

Our backend and messaging providers may process data outside India. Where that happens, we rely on the providers’ contractual safeguards and standard data protection terms.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our service or the law. The “Last updated” date at the top will change, and material changes will be announced on this page.
